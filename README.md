### 🧠 About me

- 🔭 I build **Somali-language generative AI** — a custom **GPT-2 trained from scratch**, fine-tuned for **summarization** and **image captioning** (ViT-B/32 + Q-Former), plus the **T5 family** (mT5, Flan-T5).
- 🛠️ I build the **data side** too — APIs and scrapers that collect Somali text from the web & social media, plus **data labeling** to train models in a low-resource language.
- 📄 **Co-author** of *"User-Friendly Web-Based Deep Learning System for TB and Pneumonia Diagnosis in Developing Countries"* — published in **IEEE**.
- 🌐 I'm also a **full-stack** (React, Node, Django) and **mobile** (Flutter) developer, so I ship models end-to-end.
- 💬 Ask me about **Generative AI, LLMs, Computer Vision, Python, React, and Flutter**.
- 📫 Reach me at **kabuur7@gmail.com**
